Improving Sales of a Local Shoe Store

This project shows how digital marketing can be used to promote a local shoe store, reach the right customers and increase enquiries and sales through online platforms.

The website focuses on SEO, social media marketing, online advertising and email marketing.

Overall Targeting Approach

The store can use different digital platforms according to customer interests. Instagram can focus on students and younger customers, while Facebook and WhatsApp can help reach families and working people. Local digital advertisements can target customers around the store.

7. Business Improvement Suggestions

Suggestions for improving the shoe store using digital marketing

1. Improve Google Presence

Keep the business profile updated with correct address, contact details, photos, products, working hours and reviews.

2. Use More Reels

Create short videos showing new shoes, different sizes, offers and simple product demonstrations.

3. Collect Customer Reviews

Ask satisfied customers to leave genuine reviews after purchasing footwear.

4. Referral Offers

Give a small discount or benefit to existing customers who bring new customers to the store.

5. Simple Online Enquiry

Provide an easy WhatsApp contact so customers can ask about price, size and availability before visiting.

6. Track Marketing Results

Track social media reach, enquiries, website visits and sales to understand which marketing methods work best.
